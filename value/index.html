Value

We don’t necessarily need more ideas, but better decisions about which ideas matter.

Blue Trampoline focuses on the part of development that often remains unclear. Where to focus, what to pursue, and what something could become.

The value is not in delivering full strategies. It is in helping leaders define development in a way that can be understood, tested, and acted upon.

What we bring

  • Defining focus areas: Identifying where development is relevant in the business, within or alongside existing strategy.
  • Shaping opportunities: Turning early ideas, curiosity, or technical possibilities into structured opportunities.
  • Creating a shared language: Making development understandable across leadership and teams, not just owned by individuals.
  • Testing before committing: Replacing assumptions with structured exploration and validation.
  • Visualising and communicating: Using clear models and storytelling to make opportunities tangible.

What clients leave with

The outcome is not a concept. It is a foundation.

Clients typically leave with:

  • a clearer view of where to focus
  • a structured description of an opportunity
  • a defined starting point for further development
  • a shared understanding across key stakeholders

In many cases, this becomes the basis for a development project.

How the work takes shape

The work is practical and structured, often including:

  • workshops to define problems and opportunities
  • interviews and investigation of context
  • business model and value proposition work
