The idea is that the website thinks in Business model canvas (BMC).
The company is attacking the idea of partnerships to solve internal challenges.
The company role is to handle the interspace of the partners and the phase between pressent and fututre state.
Therefore Value is about the value proposition for the potential customer segment and should create desirability as the BMC does. The Partners section shall build bridge towards the feasibility of the challenges the customer segment might have, so instead of being one stop shop our company works in the interspace and are not locked to solve one set of challenges but are heavely dependent on our partnerships. The other way around we can add value to our partners in the sence we can handle the overall elements around their topic matter expertice and add missing elements to the mix. For our customers it should feel like they get a better playingfield.
Finally the Insights should create a space where the customers can get inspiration and understand our ways of working, the mindset is that we give away our methods and templates, plus where they come from, but we have the experience and time to execute for the customers. Then the right customers will chose us and the ones that does not want to pay can still be attracted to us.
